# Fidelia — Letters of Interest (templates)

*Templates to sign · October 2026* · [Version française](LETTERS-OF-INTEREST.fr.md)

Two non-binding letters of interest: one for a pilot merchant, one for a microfinance institution (MFI). Signed, they stand in for the traction a pre-pilot project cannot show yet: they prove that merchants and a licensed institution want to try Fidelia.

**The French version is the one people sign** ([LETTERS-OF-INTEREST.fr.md](LETTERS-OF-INTEREST.fr.md)); this English version is for investors and partners who read English.

**Before asking for a signature:**

- Fill in the fields in brackets `[…]`; promise nothing beyond what is written.
- Print two copies: one for the signer, one for Fidelia.
- Read the letter aloud to the merchant if needed, and answer their questions before they sign.
- For an MFI: email the letter to the relevant manager first, then have it signed by an authorised person (general manager or delegate).
- Never present the letter as a contract, or as a condition for getting credit.

Once signed, the letters are scanned into the data room; the investor brief states how many were signed in its "Traction" section ([INVESTOR-BRIEF.md](INVESTOR-BRIEF.md)), never the signers' personal details.

## Letter 1 — Merchant

**[Shop name]**
[Category: maquis / grocery / mini-market] · [Address or landmark], [Commune], Abidjan
Owner: [Full name] · Phone: [number]

To Fidelia
[Fidelia address] · contact.fidelia@regisse.com

Abidjan, [date]

**Subject: Letter of interest in the Fidelia pilot**

Dear Sir or Madam,

I, the undersigned [full name], owner of [shop name], state my interest in the Fidelia pilot in the commune of [Commune], for about six months.

**What Fidelia provides, free of charge during the pilot:**

- the Fidelia merchant app, which works even without a connection;
- a daily cash book that adds up my mobile-money payments and my cash sales;
- a loyalty programme: my customers earn points with me and I give them rewards I choose;
- a place on the map of the Fidelia customer app, where I can publish offers;
- the history of my sales, which belongs to me.

**What I am willing to try:**

- recording my sales in the app, while still being paid on my own mobile-money wallet (Wave, Orange Money, MTN MoMo or Moov), at no extra cost;
- publishing offers and, when a customer comes with an offer, noting whether they are a new customer;
- giving my feedback to the Fidelia team in two meetings during the pilot.

**What I understand:**

- The pilot is free. At the end, Fidelia will offer me a paid plan (around XOF 5,000 to 10,000 a month); I will be free to accept it or not.
- My customers' money goes straight to my wallet: Fidelia never holds my money.
- Fidelia does not share my data without my consent. My history may one day help me show my business to a financial institution, but Fidelia promises no credit.

This letter expresses interest. It creates no obligation for me or for Fidelia, and I can leave the pilot at any time.

Signed in Abidjan on [date], in two copies.

Signature and shop stamp:

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

[Full name]

## Letter 2 — Microfinance institution (MFI)

**[Institution name]**
Licence no. [number] · [Address], Abidjan
Contact: [Name, position] · [email] · [phone]

To Fidelia
[Fidelia address] · contact.fidelia@regisse.com

Abidjan, [date]

**Subject: Letter of interest in a pilot of consented sales histories**

Dear Sir or Madam,

[Institution name], a licensed microfinance institution, has learned about Fidelia, a solution that turns the daily sales of small merchants (maquis, grocery shops) into a verified business history that the merchant owns and, with consent, can present to a licensed institution.

We hereby express our interest in exploring with Fidelia:

1. **an activity statement**, signed and consented (monthly turnover, regularity, share of sales confirmed by the payment operator), as one input when assessing merchants who apply to us;
2. **a limited pilot** with 5 to 10 volunteer merchants, current or prospective clients of our institution, in the commune of [Commune];
3. **a monitoring tool**, with each merchant's consent, for the business activity of merchants we have financed.

**We acknowledge that:**

- Fidelia holds no funds, grants no credit and guarantees no repayment; the credit decision remains entirely ours;
- no data will be passed to us without the written, revocable consent of the merchant concerned, nor before a data-sharing agreement complying with Law No. 2013-450 on personal data protection and ARTCI requirements is signed;
- until that agreement, exchanges will use synthetic or anonymised data.

The terms of any partnership (scope, financial conditions, duration, data security) will be set out in a separate agreement.

This letter expresses interest. It is neither a financial commitment, nor a contract, nor exclusivity, for either party. Its content and the discussions that follow are confidential.

Signed in Abidjan on [date], in two copies.

For [Institution name]:

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

[Name, position], signature and stamp

For Fidelia:

&nbsp;
