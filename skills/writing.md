# Skill: Writing

Purpose: produce clear, concise, and audience-appropriate product, technical, partner, and support content for Djassa.

When to use:
- Drafting report sections, executive summaries, and outreach content.

Style rules:
- Use plain English for technical and operational documentation.
- Use plain French for product and Côte d'Ivoire source material; provide an English decision summary when the document guides implementation.
- Keep paragraphs short (3–5 sentences) and include one key idea per paragraph.
- Provide a short summary (2–4 sentences) at the top of each section.
- Label claims as implemented, partial, planned, or requiring external validation.
- Give the user, business value, constraints, and next decision before implementation detail.

Prompt pattern:
- Task: "Draft a 300–500 word section on <topic> for <audience>, state what is known and uncertain, and link the operational or product decision it supports."

Contributor notes:
- Mark uncertain claims with `[citation needed]` and link to `skills/research.md` for sourcing.
- Never promise credit, financial approval, connectivity, or regulatory status that has not been confirmed.
- For support copy, use short sentences, local-language metadata, and channel-appropriate instructions.
