# Skill: Research

Purpose: conduct structured, evidence-based research on financial inclusion, payments, connectivity, local markets, and partner ecosystems relevant to Djassa.

When to use:
- Preparing literature reviews or background sections.
- Sourcing statistics, reports, and primary data on inclusion financière.
- Comparing country requirements, payment rails, languages, and support channels.
- Preparing partner, regulatory, or pilot research.

Recommended outputs:
- Short annotated bibliography (title, author, year, 2-sentence summary, relevance).
- Data source list with license and access notes.
- Country comparison table with source date and confidence level.
- Explicit distinction between verified fact, estimate, assumption, and product hypothesis.

Prompt pattern:
- Task: "Find authoritative sources on <topic> (since <year>), summarize key findings, and list datasets with access links and licenses."

Contributor notes:
- Prefer official sources (World Bank, IMF, national statistics, peer-reviewed journals).
- Record search queries and date retrieved.
- Verify country-specific claims against current official sources before product or partner decisions.
- Do not generalize one African market to the continent.
- Record regulatory uncertainty and identify the institution or counsel that must confirm it.
