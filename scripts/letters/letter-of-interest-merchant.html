Subject: Letter of interest in the Fidelia pilot

Dear Sir or Madam,

I, the undersigned , owner of the shop above, state my interest in the Fidelia pilot in my commune, for about six months.

What Fidelia provides, free of charge during the pilot

  • the Fidelia merchant app, which works even without a connection;
  • a daily cash book that adds up my mobile-money payments and my cash sales;
  • a loyalty programme: my customers earn points with me and I give them rewards I choose;
  • a place on the map of the Fidelia customer app, where I can publish offers;
  • the history of my sales, which belongs to me.

What I am willing to try

  • recording my sales in the app, while still being paid on my own mobile-money wallet (Wave, Orange Money, MTN MoMo or Moov), at no extra cost;
  • publishing offers and, when a customer comes with an offer, noting whether they are a new customer;
  • giving my feedback to the Fidelia team in two meetings during the pilot.

What I understand

  • The pilot is free. At the end, Fidelia will offer me a paid plan (around XOF 5,000 to 10,000 a month); I will be free to accept it or not.
  • My customers' money goes straight to my wallet: Fidelia never holds my money.
  • Fidelia does not share my data without my consent. My history may one day help me show my business to a financial institution, but Fidelia promises no credit.

This letter expresses interest. It creates no obligation for me or for Fidelia, and I can leave the pilot at any time.
