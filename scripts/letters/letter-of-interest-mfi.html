Subject: Letter of interest in a pilot of consented sales histories

Dear Sir or Madam,

Our licensed microfinance institution has learned about Fidelia, a solution that turns the daily sales of small merchants (maquis, grocery shops) into a verified business history that the merchant owns and, with consent, can present to a licensed institution.

We hereby express our interest in exploring with Fidelia:

  1. an activity statement, signed and consented (monthly turnover, regularity, share of sales confirmed by the payment operator), as one input when assessing merchants who apply to us;
  2. a limited pilot with 5 to 10 volunteer merchants, current or prospective clients of our institution, in the commune of ;
  3. a monitoring tool, with each merchant's consent, for the business activity of merchants we have financed.

We acknowledge that

  • Fidelia holds no funds, grants no credit and guarantees no repayment; the credit decision remains entirely ours;
  • no data will be passed to us without the written, revocable consent of the merchant concerned, nor before a data-sharing agreement complying with Law No. 2013-450 on personal data protection and ARTCI requirements is signed;
  • until that agreement, exchanges will use synthetic or anonymised data.

The terms of any partnership (scope, financial conditions, duration, data security) will be set out in a separate agreement.

This letter expresses interest. It is neither a financial commitment, nor a contract, nor exclusivity, for either party. Its content and the discussions that follow are confidential.
